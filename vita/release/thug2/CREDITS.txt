THUG2 - Tony Hawk's Underground 2, running natively on the PS Vita
Not emulated, rebuilt: the game engine is recompiled for the Vita's ARM CPU,
with a graphics backend written on top of vitaGL.

This package contains no game data. You need your own copy of the Xbox game.
